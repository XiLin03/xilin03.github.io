### Model Card

I am an M.S. student in Robotics at Johns Hopkins University and currently a WAM research intern at Huawei/Yinwang Automotive BU.

My recent work focuses on **world-action models for embodied agents**, especially pretraining pipelines for **dexterous-hand manipulation**. I am interested in how egocentric manipulation videos can be converted into action-sensitive supervision for future prediction, latent dynamics, and robot decision-making.

Previously, I worked on long-horizon embodied navigation at JD Explore Academy, humanoid action data and simulation at Xiaomi Robotics, and robot dynamics / terradynamics at Johns Hopkins University.
