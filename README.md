## About

I am an M.S. student in Robotics at Johns Hopkins University. Previously, I was an Embodied Foundation WAM Pretraining Intern at Yinwang. I am currently looking for Ph.D. opportunities for Spring/Fall 2027 admission and working on research related to world-action models (WAMs) and vision-language-action (VLA) systems. I welcome collaborations in any form - feel free to reach out.

## Selected Work

* **BAT-Nav: Belief-Based Arbitration and Termination via Remaining Discoverability in Multi-Goal Semantic Navigation** — *RAL 2026, Under Review, First Author*
  Budget-conditioned goal arbitration for multi-goal semantic navigation under a shared action budget.

* **Dual-Anchoring** — *ECCV 2026, Second Author*
  State-drift reduction for vision-language navigation through world-model-supervised anchoring.

* **Action-Sensitive WAM Pretraining** — *Ongoing*
  Building an egocentric manipulation pretraining pipeline for dexterous-hand videos, latent dynamics, VLM-assisted supervision, and future prediction.
