## Model Card

I am an M.S. student in Robotics at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/) and an Embodied AI / WAM research intern at Huawei Automotive BU, working with Dr. [Zhaowen Li](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=ID_HERE).

My current research focuses on **World Action Models (WAMs)** for embodied agents, especially action-sensitive pretraining for **dexterous-hand manipulation**. I am interested in how egocentric manipulation videos can be converted into structured supervision for future prediction, latent dynamics, and robot decision-making.

Previously, I worked on long-horizon embodied navigation at JD Explore Academy, humanoid action data and simulation at Xiaomi Robotics, and robot dynamics / terradynamics at Johns Hopkins University.

## Latent Representation

My background started from mechanical engineering and robot systems, then gradually moved toward embodied AI, robot learning, and world models. I like research problems that sit between **modeling**, **control**, and **decision-making**: not only making robots see the world, but also helping them predict what will happen after they act.

## Selected Checkpoints

- **MORN**  
  *Metacognitive Object-Goal Regulation for Resource-Rational Long-Horizon Navigation*  
  *RAL Under Review · First Author*  

  A long-horizon ObjectNav framework that adds a metacognitive executive layer for deciding when to continue, switch, backtrack, or terminate under resource constraints.

- **Dual-Anchoring**  
  *Addressing State Drift in Vision-Language Navigation*  
  *ECCV 2026 · Second Author*  

  A long-horizon VLN framework that reduces state drift through instruction-progress anchoring and world-model-supervised landmark anchoring.

- **Action-Semantic WAM Pretraining**  
  *Ongoing Research at Huawei Automotive BU*  

  A WAM pretraining pipeline that converts egocentric dexterous-hand videos into video chunks, latent representations, prompt variants, and action-sensitive supervision for future prediction.
